Fragment's flat rate

Fragment sells any amount from 50 Stars at $0.015 a Star, paid in Gram (TON) or a stablecoin. The App Store sells fixed packs only and adds the store's cut, so the same Stars cost more there. A Star earned by a channel or bot withdraws at $0.013; bought Stars never withdraw.

Questions

Where are Telegram Stars cheapest?

On Fragment, at $0.015 a Star in every country. Among App Store countries, compare the per-Star column: it differs a lot between currencies.

Why do App Store and Google Play prices differ from Fragment?

The app stores take a cut and sell fixed packs; Fragment is Telegram's own marketplace and charges the same rate for any amount. Google Play publishes no price list, so this dataset has none.

Are these prices converted with an exchange rate?

No. Each App Store price is the store's own figure in its own currency. Only the Fragment column is in dollars.
