Telegram Gifts & Stars Dataset

Dated, sourced figures on Telegram Stars, Telegram Premium and Telegram Gifts, as CSV and JSON you can download, chart or cite.

Data updated: 2026-11-08

On Fragment a Telegram Star costs $0.015 (1,000 Stars = $15.00); in the US App Store 1,000 Stars cost $19.99. Telegram Premium is cheapest in Indonesia (Rp 59.000 a month) of the 15 App Store countries read. 123 collectible gift collections hold 1,493,721 items; the rarest, Intelligence Cups, has 28.

Stars prices

124 App Store pack prices in 18 countries, plus Fragment's flat rate.

How the data is collected

  • Prices are read, not converted. Each App Store price is the figure Apple prints on Telegram's listing in that country, in that country's currency, on the date in the checked column.
  • Fragment (Telegram's own marketplace) prices come from fragment.com in US dollars.
  • Issued counts of collectible gifts come from Fragment's gift pages. They are readings: a collection's count can still grow.
  • Limited gifts come from the public log @GiftChanges, which began on 24 October 2024. Gifts sold before then have no release date.
  • Dollar estimates (*_usd_estimate) use the central-bank rates in fx-rates.json, never a live rate.

Questions

Is this an official Telegram dataset?

No. Gift Club reads Telegram's public sources (the App Store, Fragment, the Bot API and the public gift log) and records each figure with its date and source link. Telegram publishes no such list itself.

Can I use the data in an article, app or research?

Yes. The data is CC BY 4.0: reuse it anywhere, commercially too, as long as you credit Gift Club and link to this dataset.

How much is one Telegram Star in dollars?

$0.015 on Fragment at any amount from 50 Stars, and about $0.0199 in a 100-Star pack in the US App Store. A Star a channel earns pays out $0.013.

How often is it updated?

Each table carries the date it was read. When a price or count changes, the row is re-read at its source and the date moves with it.
