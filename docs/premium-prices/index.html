What Telegram Premium costs in 15 App Store countries, cheapest first.

Questions

Which country has the cheapest Telegram Premium?

Indonesia, at Rp 59.000 a month (about $3.26), of the countries in this dataset.

How many Stars does a Premium gift cost?

1,000 Stars for 3 months and 2,500 for 12 months, fixed by Telegram.

Why is there no Google Play column?

Google Play shows prices only inside the app, on a signed-in device, so there is no public page to read and cite.
